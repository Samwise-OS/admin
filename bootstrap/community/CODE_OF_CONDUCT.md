# Collaboration standards

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss the work and its evidence; avoid harassment,
threats, discrimination, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private
information. Give actionable feedback and respect maintainer scope decisions.

Maintainers may moderate or restrict participation to protect constructive work.
For conduct concerns, contact the repository's listed maintainer using an available
private channel. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. If a private channel
is not listed, request contact without including the sensitive details.
